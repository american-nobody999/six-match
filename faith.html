Friday Prayer صلاة الجمعة

Dear Stars in the Sky,
Thank you for the blessing you gave the world that is Palestine.
Thank you for every single beautiful moment you allow us to experience with them. Those beautiful, raw, truthful moments of a great people who are so deep in the darkness of pain, loss, and grief, yet still radiate the brightest light of hope, love, and resilience.
I always want to start with this.
Is it sunset time? The week has passed. It is Friday. You are here, creator, and I am here, and all your beloved and believers, and the broken of humanity, and the fearful and hesitant, we are here, aren't we?
Oh greatest of beings, and wisest of beings, and most patient and forgiving, how every hardship is such a blessing from you, how grateful I am that your lessons and guidance are so perfect and so painfully beautiful, how grateful I am that we are here to witness and observe and learn from all of this, how grateful I am that we are here to be with you in this moment.
You are so paitent with me, all I need to do is ask, and you will give, all I need to do is be open, and you will guide me to find the way. You pour signs into my soul, and it becomes the truth, the light which I follow. I am so grateful that you have given us the way already, and that we just need to be open to find it.
I am so lucky, so blessed in this life, I am American, even without any wealth I still live a life of luxury compared to most of the world, compared to our beloved people.
None of it does any good in this life, because, comforts and material things are not going to the grave with me. It will not buffer me from the accounting of my deeds from this life or who I chose to be. You are everything. You give everything. You take away what you will, that does not serve us in our journey to you. We all have our parts to play, good and bad, we all have path we will take, and we will speak of it to you when the time comes.
I cannot wash the blood of the people off my hands, I cannot bring them back, you took them to be with you, so much peace and blessings to them, although I think the real believers would tell you keep all the gold and riches promised, being with you is the greatest reward.
I stand with the people, I will continue to stand with the people, I will continue to fight for the people, nothing will change my faith in you or my love for you, even when I bear witness to what is done to your people. I don't understand it. This is so senseless. This is so evil. My heart stops so often throughout the week, and it take a moment with you for it to start beating again, and I am but a witness. There are the people themselves, families, friends, every single human on this planet is hurt by the state of beloved Palestine in some way right now. They just don't see.
I ask forgiveness for that. I ask for our forgiveness for getting to a point of numbness and disassociation as a society, we even allow that. I ask forgiveness for even having to show a burning Palestian family death to the western American public to get them to think of beloved Palestine. I ask forgivenesss for all of humanity, and that you stay with us in our pain, that we remain calm, peaceful, and we keep working together.
whats my daily dua? My prayer? The same, everyday, because it started there, and it shall end there, Free the State of Palestine, free the people, give them their rightful existance in the world, on all stages, all platforms. I would ask they had the same rights as an American.
And the saddest part, oh the saddest part, courage to face this week ahead, until next Friday. Please bring us closer together, allow us to be returned to a point we are not scattered, and we are together, we can go about doing great things instead of digging mass graves, an equal future for all of us.
Thank you for the leaders who have shown courage, who have demonstrated patience and endure purscecution for the people. May they sleep well tonight know they have the support and the love of the people they are trying to fight for. May they know we see them doing the right thing.
We move forward, shall we go to work?
I love you so much, and I am still here.

لا أستطيع غسل دماء الشعب عن يديّ، ولا أستطيع إعادتهم، أنت أخذتهم ليكونوا معك، فسلام وبركات عليهم، وأظن أن المؤمنين الحقيقيين سيقولون لك: احتفظ بكل الذهب والغنائم الموعودة، فالقرب منك هو أعظم الجزاء.
أنا أقف مع الشعب، وسأستمر في الوقوف مع الشعب، وسأستمر في النضال من أجل الشعب، لن يغير شيء إيماني بك أو حبي لك، حتى حين أشهد ما يُفعل بشعبك. لا أفهم ذلك. هذا أمر بلا معنى. هذا شر مطلق. قلبي يتوقف كثيرًا خلال الأسبوع، ويحتاج إلى لحظة معك حتى يعود للنبض، وأنا لست إلا شاهدًا. هناك الشعب نفسه، العائلات، الأصدقاء، كل إنسان على هذا الكوكب متأثر بحالة فلسطين الحبيبة بطريقة ما الآن. هم فقط لا يرون.
أسأل المغفرة على ذلك. أسأل مغفرتنا لأننا وصلنا إلى مرحلة الخدر والانفصال كمجتمع، بل ونسمح بذلك. أسأل المغفرة حتى على اضطرارنا إلى إظهار عائلة فلسطينية تحترق حتى الموت لكي يفكر الجمهور الأمريكي الغربي بفلسطين الحبيبة. أسأل المغفرة لكل البشرية، وأن تبقى معنا في ألمنا، وأن نبقى هادئين وسلميين، وأن نستمر في العمل معًا.
ما هو دعائي اليومي؟ صلاتي؟ الشيء نفسه، كل يوم، لأنه بدأ هناك، وسينتهي هناك: حرروا دولة فلسطين، حرروا الشعب، أعطوهم وجودهم المشروع في العالم، على كل المستويات، وعلى كل المنصات. كنت أتمنى أن يحصلوا على الحقوق نفسها التي يتمتع بها الأمريكي.

شكرًا للقادة الذين أظهروا شجاعة، وصبروا واحتملوا الاضطهاد من أجل الشعب. ليتناموا الليلة بهدوء عارفين أن لديهم دعم وحب الشعب الذين يناضلون من أجله. ليتنبهوا أننا نراهم يفعلون الصواب.
